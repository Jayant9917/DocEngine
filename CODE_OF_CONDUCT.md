# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the DocEngine project respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and welcome different perspectives.
- Give feedback focused on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to correct them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwelcome sexual
attention, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the
contact method listed on the project's GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed respectfully and kept confidential where possible.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
